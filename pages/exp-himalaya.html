About Himalayan Treks

The Indian Himalayas are home to some of the world's most spectacular trekking terrain — from the meadow-hopping trails of Kashmir's Lidder Valley to the stark, moonlike landscapes of Ladakh and Spiti. With peaks exceeding 7,000 metres and passes over 5,000 metres, these mountains offer adventures for every level of fitness and ambition.

The Valley of Flowers in Uttarakhand, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, erupts with over 500 species of alpine wildflowers in July and August. The Markha Valley Trek in Ladakh crosses five high passes with views of Kang Yatse and the stark beauty of the Zanskar Range. The Chadar Trek in January, across the frozen Zanskar River, is considered one of the world's most extraordinary winter adventures.

Tips for Your Experience

Acclimatise for at least 2 full days before attempting passes above 4000m
Hire a registered guide and porter — it supports local communities and improves safety
The best trekking months are May–June and September–October; July–August brings monsoon to most routes
Travel insurance with helicopter evacuation cover is essential for remote treks
Pack layers — temperatures can swing 30°C between midday and midnight at altitude
