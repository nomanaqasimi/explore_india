About Varanasi

One of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities, Varanasi (also known as Kashi or Benares) has been a centre of learning and civilisation for over 3,000 years. Situated on the banks of the sacred River Ganga, it is the holiest of the seven sacred cities in Hinduism and a place where millions come to seek moksha — liberation from the cycle of rebirth.

The ghats — broad stone steps descending to the river — are the heartbeat of Varanasi. Dasaswamedh Ghat erupts each evening in the spectacular Ganga Aarti ceremony, a choreographed fire ritual of mesmerising beauty. Manikarnika Ghat, the main cremation ghat, burns around the clock. Narrow lanes behind the ghats are lined with silk weavers, tea stalls, and ancient temples. Dawn boat rides on the Ganga offer an unparalleled perspective.

Travel Tips

Stay in a guesthouse on or near the ghats for the full atmosphere
Dress modestly — many areas are active places of worship
A private boat at dawn costs around ₹300–500 for one hour
Visit Sarnath (10 km away) where the Buddha gave his first sermon
The lanes of the old city are best explored on foot with a local guide
