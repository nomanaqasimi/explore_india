About Yoga & Wellness

India is the birthplace of yoga — a practice over 5,000 years old — and no country on earth offers a more authentic and transformative wellness experience. From Himalayan ashrams to Kerala Ayurvedic resorts, the range of experiences is staggering, and the depth of tradition is unmatched anywhere in the world.

Rishikesh, nestled in the foothills of the Himalayas on the banks of the Ganges, is the undisputed world capital of yoga. The Beatles came here in 1968 to study with Maharishi Mahesh Yogi, and today hundreds of ashrams offer everything from week-long immersions to month-long teacher training certifications. Kerala's Ayurvedic tradition — a 3,000-year-old system of holistic medicine — offers panchakarma detox treatments, herbal oil massages, and rejuvenation programmes.

Tips for Your Experience

Book Rishikesh retreats at least 1–2 months ahead for the March festival period
Authentic Ayurvedic centres will have a doctor consultation before any treatment
Many ashrams require a minimum stay of 7–10 days for meaningful immersion
Alcohol and non-vegetarian food are restricted in most ashrams — plan accordingly
Look for Kerala Government-certified Ayurveda resorts for genuine treatments
