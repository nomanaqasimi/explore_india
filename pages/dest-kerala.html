About Kerala Backwaters

Stretching over 900 km along Kerala's coast, the backwaters are a serene network of lagoons, lakes, rivers, and canals that run parallel to the Arabian Sea. Fringed by swaying coconut palms and dotted with rice paddies and tiny fishing villages, this is India at its most lushly tranquil — a world that moves at the pace of a slow-drifting kettuvallam (houseboat).

Alleppey (Alappuzha) is the gateway to the backwaters and the starting point for most houseboat cruises. Spend a night on a traditional rice barge converted into a floating villa, waking to the sound of kingfishers and the scent of fresh coconut toddy. The backwaters are also home to traditional fishing communities, Chinese fishing nets, Kathakali dance performances, and Ayurvedic wellness resorts.

Travel Tips

Book houseboats at least 2 weeks in advance during December–January peak season
A shared shikara (small boat) tour costs much less than a private houseboat
Carry insect repellent — mosquitoes emerge at dusk near the water
Negotiate directly with houseboat owners at Alleppey boat jetty for best rates
The Nehru Trophy Boat Race in August is a spectacular annual event
