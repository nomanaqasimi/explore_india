About Jaipur

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, earns its nickname 'The Pink City' from the terracotta-pink hue of its old walled city — painted in 1876 to welcome Prince Albert. Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Jai Singh II, it was one of the first planned cities in India, its streets laid out on an ancient Vastu Shastra grid.

From the fairy-tale Amber Fort perched on a hill above Maota Lake, to the five-storey Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds) whose 953 small windows allowed royal ladies to observe street life unseen, Jaipur overwhelms with grandeur. Add world-class bazaars selling block-print textiles, blue pottery, and gem-encrusted jewellery, and you have one of India's most complete travel experiences.

Travel Tips

Buy a composite ticket covering Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, and Jantar Mantar
Auto-rickshaws are plentiful — agree on a fare before you get in
Block-print fabric from Sanganeri and Bagru villages makes a great souvenir
The rooftop restaurants near Hawa Mahal offer stunning views at dusk
Jaipur Literature Festival (January) is the world's largest free literary festival
